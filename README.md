# 🎓 StudySphere AI

StudySphere AI is an AI-powered learning platform that helps students organize their studies, review course materials, practice with quizzes, and receive personalized tutoring through an intelligent AI assistant.

## 📌 Problem Statement

Many students struggle to manage study materials, understand difficult concepts, and prepare effectively for exams. They often switch between multiple apps for notes, quizzes, and AI assistance.

StudySphere AI solves this problem by providing an all-in-one learning platform where students can organize their studies and receive AI-powered educational support in one place.

**Target Users**
- University students
- College students
- Self-learners
- Anyone preparing for exams

# 🤖 AI Feature

## AI Tutor

The application includes an AI Tutor that helps students:

- Explain difficult concepts
- Answer academic questions
- Generate practice quizzes
- Summarize study topics
- Assist with programming questions
- Help prepare for exams
- Provide step-by-step explanations

### AI System Instructions

The AI tutor is instructed to:

- Prioritize accuracy over speed.
- Teach concepts instead of only giving answers.
- Explain solutions step-by-step.
- Adapt explanations to the student's knowledge level.
- Use clear and simple language.
- Generate quizzes when appropriate.
- End every response with:
  > "What next do you want from me?"
